🩹 First Aid

  • For severe bleeding, apply firm, continuous pressure with a clean dressing.
  • For a serious burn, cool the area with cool running water. Do not apply ice.
  • If someone is unconscious or not breathing normally, seek emergency medical help.

🆘 Emergency Checklist

  • Move away from immediate danger if safe.
  • Call emergency services when possible.
  • Share your location with responders.
  • Follow instructions from trained responders.
